Before you visit

Questions we hear often

Walk-ins, hours, parking, braces and kids’ care — the short answers before you WhatsApp us.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Yes. Klinik Pergigian Dentaluna in Sungai Besi welcomes walk-ins all day, and you can also book ahead on WhatsApp or by calling 014-660 6010.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Thursday 10am–10pm, Saturday and Sunday 10am–6pm. Closed on Friday.

Where is the clinic in Sungai Besi?

22-G, Jalan Tasik Utama 10, Taman Tasik Damai, Sungai Besi, 57000 Kuala Lumpur. We are about a 5-minute walk from MRT Sungai Besi, opposite De Gift House and KawanMu / MU Pusat Pakaian Sekolah. Patient parking is reserved.

Do you offer braces and aligners?

Yes. We plan braces and aligner treatment with the same doctor throughout, with unhurried appointments. Message us on WhatsApp to talk through options.

Do you treat children?

Yes. We provide kids’ dentistry and have a dedicated corner for younger patients.

Do you have dental X-rays?

Yes. Dental X-rays, including 3D CBCT and lateral ceph imaging, are available when clinically indicated for treatment planning.
